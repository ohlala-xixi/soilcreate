# Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

SoilCreate respects the privacy of visitors, customers, distributors, and engineering partners who use this website.

## Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry, subscribe to updates, download resources, or contact us, we may collect business contact information such as your name, company name, work email, destination country, WhatsApp number, and project requirements.

We may also collect basic website usage data through analytics tools, including visited pages, traffic source, browser type, device type, and approximate location.

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- Respond to product and project inquiries.
- Provide quotations, technical information, and product documentation.
- Improve website content, navigation, and lead-response workflows.
- Understand which products, case studies, and technical resources are useful to visitors.
- Send relevant business updates if you have subscribed or requested follow-up.

## Inquiry Forms and Email

Inquiry and subscription forms on this website may be processed through third-party form services. Form submissions are delivered to SoilCreate's business email for sales and technical follow-up.

We do not sell personal or business contact information.

## Analytics and Cookies

This website may use Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics, and similar tools to understand website performance and visitor behavior. These tools may use cookies or comparable technologies.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

## Data Sharing

We may share necessary inquiry details internally with sales, engineering, logistics, or support personnel in order to respond to your request. We may also share information with service providers that help operate the website, process forms, host files, or analyze traffic.

## Data Retention

We retain inquiry records and business communication history for as long as reasonably necessary to support project follow-up, customer service, compliance, and internal business records.

## Your Choices

You may contact us to request correction or removal of your business contact information from our active communication records, subject to reasonable business and legal retention requirements.
