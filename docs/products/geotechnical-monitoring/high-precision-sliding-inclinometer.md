The SoilCreate High-Precision Bluetooth Sliding Inclinometer is a portable deep-displacement monitoring system for manual borehole inclinometer surveys. The probe slides inside standard 70 mm grooved inclinometer casing and records inclination at 0.5 m intervals. Each segment contributes a horizontal displacement component, and the full displacement profile is reconstructed from the bottom datum upward.

Compared with traditional two-person readout workflows, the Bluetooth system links the probe reel, mobile app, field curve review, checksum control, and cloud synchronization into one process. It is suitable for contractors who need fast, repeatable borehole surveys in foundation pits, slopes, dams, tunnels, bridges, and retaining structures.

For a quotation or configuration check, share borehole depth, casing diameter, expected survey frequency, required cable length, single-axis or dual-axis preference, and whether cloud reporting or calibration documentation is required.
