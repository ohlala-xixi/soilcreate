The SoilCreate In-Place Inclinometer (IPI) is designed for automated deep-displacement monitoring in boreholes where manual surveys cannot provide enough time resolution. A chain of inclinometer segments is installed in the borehole and connected to a data logger or telemetry gateway, giving engineers continuous access to movement trends by depth.

The system is especially useful during excavation, tunneling, slope stabilization, dam safety observation, and rail-transit construction, where rapid deformation may occur between manual survey visits. During installation, each segment is mapped to its depth so later abnormal readings can be traced directly to a specific node.

For procurement review, share borehole depth, casing size, sensor spacing, required sampling interval, power supply condition, telemetry preference, cloud platform needs, and expected alarm thresholds.
